I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the Institute of Natural Sciences, Westlake Institute for Advanced Study, working with [Hong Qian](https://lifesciences.westlake.edu.cn/faculty/hong-qian.html) in Qian Lab. My current work focuses on statistical inference for molecular dynamics, using fluorescence correlation spectroscopy and photon-counting data.

I received my Ph.D. in Biostatistics from [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu/), advised by [Weng Kee Wong](https://www.biostat.ucla.edu/people/wong) and [Gang Li](https://gang-li.ph.ucla.edu/).
