# Public bibliography audit — 2026-09-16

## Scope

The user requested a fuller personal publications page, alongside the corrected
CIS / Qian Lab research affiliation and the supplied business card.
Public primary records were used; private manuscripts, HR files, and working
research folders were not used as publication evidence.

The resulting catalog contains 28 display entries: 10 journal/conference articles,
16 preprints/working papers, and 2 long-form works. It is a verified expansion,
not a guarantee of exhaustive coverage. No citation counts or acceptance claims
were inferred from search-result rankings.

## Editorial decisions

- Journal/conference records are separate from preprints. Published articles'
  companion arXiv, medRxiv, bioRxiv, and Research Square records are not duplicated.
- Author order and spelling follow the displayed source version, including
  Han Cui, Elvis Cui, Elvis Han Cui, Eliuvish Han Cui, and Eliuvish Cuicizion.
  Older search snippets can have superseded names/titles; current landing pages
  take precedence. In particular, arXiv:2405.12390 v4 has a different byline from v1.
- Preprint years mean first posting, not latest revision. Revised titles are used
  for KS, Brownian motion, counting processes, metaheuristics, and Markov renewal;
  earlier recognizable titles appear in notes.
- The 2026 SSRN Cuckoo Search record has its own current title and three-author
  byline. The four-author 2025 Research Square work is linked and explicitly
  called a *related manuscript*, not asserted to be an identical version.
  It is not counted as an additional display entry.
- The Statistical Compass is labeled a textbook/monograph **working manuscript**,
  not a publisher-issued book. The UCLA dissertation is a separate long-form work.
- Old CV work-in-preparation claims without an independently verified current
  record were not promoted to publications.
- Google Scholar could not be retrieved in this environment. ResearchGate and
  DBLP were used for discovery only, then matched to primary records below.
